The Fox and the Dog

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This classic sentence contains every letter of the English alphabet.

Why It Matters

In typography, this sentence has been used since the late 1800s to test typewriters and fonts. It's simple, memorable, and surprisingly useful.

A fox is a wild animal that is known for being clever and quick on its feet.

Don't forget: the dog was lazy, but the fox didn't care. They had an arrangement.
